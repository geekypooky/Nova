Title: Puberty can be difficult for kids. And when ADHD is in the mix, things can be even tougher.

Key points:
- Bodies are changing, so kids need new routines (e.g. remembering hygiene products, deodorant). Use checklists and "go bags".
- There’s so much more to remember: social things, more homework, changing schedules. Accommodations and organization strategies are critical.
- Friendships are more complicated: Kids with ADHD are often less mature than peers and may struggle with complex social cues, leaving them feeling left behind.
- Bigger emotions: Hormones wreak havoc on moods, and kids with ADHD are highly sensitive to rejection (RSD). Medication effectiveness can also shift during puberty.
- They need space and support to explore identity, but still require strategies for safe independence to combat impulsivity.
