Title: Teens with ADHD and falling in love

Key points:
- Brain development: Kids with ADHD have delayed executive function development (1-3 years behind peers), making them less emotionally mature and more impulsive. They may feel they aren't ready for romance, which is perfectly normal.
- Intense emotions and hyperfocus: Teens with ADHD may hyperfocus on a new crush or relationship, letting school and friends take a backseat. Set priorities and house rules (e.g., homework before texting). If a relationship ends, feelings of loss are often overwhelming and experienced more intensely. Validate their feelings.
- Impulsivity and risky behavior: They might rush into relationships or take risks to gain attention. They are more likely to become sexually active younger, use less protection, and face unplanned pregnancies due to poor impulse control. Have frank conversations to set clear guardrails and boundaries.
