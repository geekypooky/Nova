Title: Hyperfocus: What the rise of fidget toys means for neurodivergence
Journalist Anna North explains how fidget toys went from gag gifts to a generational obsession, and what that shift reveals about neurodivergence, attention, and play.

Key points:
- Fidgeting helps neurodivergent individuals (ADHD/Autism) stay alert and regulated.
- Sensory play provides tactile experiences lacking in digital/screen-based environments.
- Normalization: Toys like NeeDoh are destigmatizing fidgeting, showing Gen Z/Alpha are more comfortable expressing sensory needs.
- The commercialization/fad aspect (TikTok drops, trading) causes them to be banned in schools due to distraction, risking backlash against genuine sensory tools.
