Title: ADHD, AuDHD, and social skills: Why you feel like too much and not enough
An episode about ADHD, AuDHD, and managing emotions and social exhaustion. 
Key themes:
- Feeling like "too much" (over-sharing, interrupting, being loud or intense) due to ADHD impulsivity and lack of inhibition.
- Feeling like "not enough" (withdrawing, masking, missing social cues) leading to social exhaustion.
- Rejection Sensitive Dysphoria (RSD) deeply impacts social interactions, causing people to either mask heavily or isolate themselves.
