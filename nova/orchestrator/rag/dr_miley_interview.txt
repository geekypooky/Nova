Title: ADHD and sex, Interview with Dr. Erika Miley
Cate Osborn: Hi everybody and welcome back to "Sorry I Missed This"...
[Transcript of interview where Dr. Miley explains that neurodivergent women consume explicit fiction at higher rates. Smut raises the arousal threshold enough to sustain focus, distracts from rejection sensitivity, and provides a safe container to explore emotional vulnerability, even helping them get through household chores.]
Key points:
- Smut is an on-ramp to erotic experiences, helping transition from daily life.
- The high level of brain and body stimulation helps ADHD brains focus.
- Smut offers a safe environment, where the reader has total control, reducing rejection fear and paralysis.
- It expands sexual vocabulary and helps communicate desires to partners.
- Smut is not an addiction, and shouldn't be shamed. It can create camaraderie.
